# P3.3 required-read defect — 2026-08-04

## Observation

P3.3 requires exact two-provider coverage for every `worker-envelope-v1` neutral field and
closed exit, and its prescribed validator receives that contract through `--worker-contract`.
Its `Required Reads` list supplies only `artifacts/P1/contract-manifest-v1.json`, which gives the
contract's filename and hash, not its neutral field or exit taxonomy. The canonical source
`root/contracts/worker-envelope-v1.md` is omitted.

## Consequence

An executor complying with the closed Required Reads order cannot author or independently verify
the required coverage without inferring frozen contract semantics. That would violate P3.3's
rule to preserve unknowns and its prohibition on changing P1 outcomes.

## Required remediation

`task:plan-design 2026.08.03-ARTIFEX-SUPERVISOR P3.3` must add the canonical worker-envelope
contract and, if needed for deterministic case expectations, its frozen case/validator carriers
to P3.3's Required Reads in the exact execution order. It must not change P0/P1 contracts,
provider observations, P3.1/P3.2 source, or P3.3 scope/acceptance without a recorded decision.
