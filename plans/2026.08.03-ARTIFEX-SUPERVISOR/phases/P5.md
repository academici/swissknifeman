# P5 — Generate the business successor, run measured pilots, and prove product readiness

## Phase Context

P5 preserves terminal P4-assured G2 as immutable smoke evidence, produces one separately
owner-authorized additive business-capable G2B package generation, then turns G2B into measured
evidence without letting it choose tasks, baselines or verdict rules after seeing results. P5.1
froze archive mappings and all live trials; P5.5 owns the contract/runtime/provider/assurance
successor; only then may P5.2–P5.4 run the reversible repository, recovery and paired battle pilots.

**Scope Included:** candidate-independent trial catalog and shadow replay; exact owner-approved
business grants and per-trial admissions; one additive G2→G2B business admission/runtime/provider/
assurance generation; two bounded non-business provider capability probes; controlled disposable
repository pilots; real fresh provider workers under frozen authority; crash/resume/duplicate/
failure evidence; matched baselines; normalized economy/intervention metrics; independent
release-readiness adjudication whose RED result keeps the item open for forward repair.

**Scope Excluded:** P0–P4 history rewrite; owner dirty-tree/business/fleet/Brain/root-CLI/generated/
release mutation; package changes outside exact D108 successor scope plus D111's single schema path; archive or P5.1 carrier edits;
raw prompts/transcripts/thinking/tool payloads/environment values; credential/quota manipulation;
post-hoc task/baseline/threshold replacement; productization, tag, push, publish or release.
