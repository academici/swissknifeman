# P4.5 authority-packet contract gap — 2026-08-05

## Observation

P4.5 requires every routine OWNER row to carry controlling source, recommendation,
alternatives, blast radius, rollback, affected slices and the consequence of no decision.
The frozen P1 `authority-policy-v1` runtime projection in
`packages/artifex/lib/artifex/authority.py` produces `DECISION_KEYS` through `_decision()`.
It carries `controlling_sources`, `selected_alternative` and `rejected_alternatives`, but no
versioned `blast_radius`, `rollback`, `affected_slices` or `no_decision_consequence` fields.

## Consequence

P4.5 cannot create the promised bounded packet from strict replay without introducing those
semantic fields through free-form journal `evidence`. That would make an authority-relevant
contract unversioned and violate the frozen P1 boundary. No Artifex package code, attention
case, G2 manifest or validator was written.

## Required forward remediation

`task:plan-design 2026.08.03-ARTIFEX-SUPERVISOR P4.5` must obtain an owner D# and assign a
versioned owner for the missing packet fields, then update the relevant P1 contract/cases and
their validator before P4.5 implementation resumes. The P4.6 `Status: green` hook predicate
and P4.4 security evidence remain consumed inputs, not substitutes for this contract.

## Resolution

D102 authorized the smallest forward versioned change in this same session. The closed
authority packet now carries all missing fields plus `blocking_dependencies`; the supervisor
records an `authority_attention` event before the run reaches `OWNER_ATTENTION`, and an explicit
owner continuation/abort records the exact packet decision ID as resolved/withdrawn. The P1
authority and supervisor contracts/cases/manifests and their validators, Artifex tests, P4 G2
assurance validator and current manifest were re-run green. This finding remains the evidence of
the original gap; it is not an open blocker.
