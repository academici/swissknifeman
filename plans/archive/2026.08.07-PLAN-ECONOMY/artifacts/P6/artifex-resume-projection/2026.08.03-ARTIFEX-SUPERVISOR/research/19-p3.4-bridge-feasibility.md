## Feasibility verdict

P3.4 cannot be made executable solely by adding a process runner or a private adapter. The required
input has three mutually dependent owners: P1.2 supplies immutable slice identity and route pins;
P1.3 supplies the authority decision; P2 supplies the durable intent and scheduler sequence. The
actual terminal shapes do not close: the P1.2 manifest omits fields required by P2 scheduling, and
the frozen P1.3 mandate excludes the P3 routes. RAG:— (repo-grounded: sources above).

The safe boundary is therefore a choice, not an implementation default. A successor contract path
must define one frozen carrier consumed by P2 and P3, then revalidate all affected P1/P2 consumers
before P3.4 can bind a provider. A scope-reduction path must delete the promise of live dispatch and
reconcile P4/P5 product claims before it can be called coherent. The existing P3.4 Files, Scope and
Validation deliberately remain untouched until the owner selects one path. RAG:— (repo-grounded:
`findings/P3.4-bridge-contract-conflict-2026-08-04.md`; D34/D36–D40/D72).

## Rejected shortcut

Do not synthesize `authority_input`, numeric token/cost/turn caps, or provider eligibility from
CLI defaults, test fixtures, a nullable P1 manifest field, or an implementation constant. Such a
mapping would be an undocumented fourth contract and would let P3 alter authority or budget meaning
without a frozen producer. RAG:— (repo-grounded: frozen contracts and terminal P2 tests above).
