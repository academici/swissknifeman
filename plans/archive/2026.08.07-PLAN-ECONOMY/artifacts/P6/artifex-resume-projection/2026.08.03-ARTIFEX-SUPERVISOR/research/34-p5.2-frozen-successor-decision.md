## Conflict decomposition

The D105 carrier answers “may these exact 14 trials be attempted?”; it does not answer “can
terminal G2 parse and execute this schema?”. G2 answers the second question for P3 readiness only
and deliberately refuses both P5 business purpose and native Codex execution. Conflating those
predicates would turn owner authority into a waiver of product capability and assurance.

pre-intent reasons).

## Alternatives

1. **Rewrite P4/G2 evidence or accept D105 schema in place — rejected.** It changes the bytes
   whose terminal assurance is the P5 baseline and retroactively changes what G2 meant.
2. **Bypass Artifex admission/adapter from a plan-local pilot harness — rejected.** The resulting
   run would not measure the P4-assured candidate boundary and would duplicate the package runtime.
3. **Reuse P3 smoke authority or drop Codex/provider parity — rejected.** Both contradict frozen
   contract refusals, D105's two-provider request and P5.4 acceptance.
4. **Freeze the incompatibility and require an additive successor generation — selected.** G2
   remains immutable evidence; a new contract/version, runtime consumer, provider mapping and
   assurance manifest must be produced forward before P5.2 can be redesigned for execution.

## Frozen decision and successor requirements

- Terminal G2 remains exactly the P4.8 generation: smoke-only dispatch admission and explicit
  Codex `CAPABILITY_UNSUPPORTED` are true characteristics, not defects to erase from history.
- D105 remains a valid, bounded owner-intent carrier and its 14 files remain immutable evidence,
  but none is runtime-consumable by G2 and none authorizes a bypass.
- A successor must be additive and distinctly versioned. It must define business task/grant/
  replay/admission schema ownership, closed pre-intent refusals, exact P3-vs-P5 separation, and a
  native provider projection whose permissions/capabilities are evidenced on the pinned provider
  surface. “Use the CLI directly” and unreachable/dead argv code are not acceptance evidence.
- The successor must be implemented in `packages/artifex/` (the sole runtime SSOT), validated by
  independent positive/negative cases, and publish a new assurance/generation record that cites G2
  as immutable predecessor. P4 files/statuses/Completion Notes/journal remain untouched.
- Package/contract/provider mutation requires a new exact owner grant with finite paths and an
  explicit successor acceptance predicate. D107 records the design choice but is not that grant.
- Only after the successor is terminal may plan-design update P5.2's Inputs/Validation and
  generation pin. Until then the item remains `🔴 Blocked`; P5.3/P5.4 remain deferred and no P5
  provider call is legal.
