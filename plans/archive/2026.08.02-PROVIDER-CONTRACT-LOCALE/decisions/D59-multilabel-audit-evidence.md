## Решение

The skills audit separates typed observations, independent multi-label signals and final
recommendations. Pairwise lexical overlap remains a cheap signal; connected components expose
clusters, and no `merge > thin > dead` precedence discards coexisting evidence. Recommendations
include `insufficient-evidence`; publication/source-only/generated classes are evaluated by their
own policies. RAG:— (current code and live audit in findings/13).

## Почему

The current single verdict converts weak proxies into content actions and hides simultaneous size,
usage, dependency, publication and provider-budget defects.
