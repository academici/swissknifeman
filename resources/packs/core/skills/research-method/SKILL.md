# Research Method

Follow structured inquiry to investigate questions, analyze documents, and surface evidence for decisions.

## Rules
1. State the objective, search space, and qualification criteria for primary sources.
2. Progress through local project artifacts before consulting external knowledge.
3. Support each substantive finding with an exact reference or citation.
4. Distinguish verified facts from inferences or unverified author claims.
5. Produce concise, structured summaries that address the prompt directly.
