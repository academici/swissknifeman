# P6 — Semantic reconciliation и real-project field validation

## Phase Context

P6 сводит новые runtime semantics в typed reason codes, canonical prompts/skills/docs и
provider projections, но меняет текст «для красоты». Каждый non-code candidate должен
иметь доказанную causal chain и получает paired behavioral check на реальных
задачах corex, agentools и edufy, а не synthetic prompt test.

**Scope Included:** reason-code taxonomy; canonical Task prompts/plan-protocol/docs required by confirmed behavior; provider regeneration/parity; three-project paired field runs; final integration/security review.
**Scope Excluded:** invented benchmark prompts; writes to source consumer worktrees; merging consumer experiment changes; release/push/deploy/rollout.

> **Контракт фазы.** Потребляет: GREEN P1–P5 implementations and P1 field protocol · Производит: synchronized semantics plus real-project evidence · Граница: no synthetic behavioral claim.

> **Phase Assurance | v1.**
> Impact: public-contract
> Integration Seams: `reason-codes` · `canonical-prompts` · `provider-projections` · `corex-field` · `agentools-field` · `edufy-field`
> Live or Irreversible: yes
> Audit Intent: direct-close-eligible
> Rationale: live external model calls run only on isolated worktrees, with no consumer source mutation or deployment. Cross-provider semantics and real-project behavior need a fresh final review; owner admission is still required for any formal phase audit.

## DoR (Definition of Ready)

- [ ] P1–P5 terminal GREEN and their result maps frozen.
- [ ] Exact non-code candidates have defect→mechanism→behavior rows; zero candidates is permitted and explicit.
- [ ] Three real tasks, commits, isolation and invalid-run rules satisfy P1 field protocol.
- [ ] Consumer source worktree states are snapshotted and protected.

## Phase Acceptance

- Reason codes distinguish schema-invalid, evidence-missing, evidence-stale, evidence-forged and policy-denied where P1 confirmed the need.
- Canonical prompts/docs/projections agree with runtime and contain no unsupported security promise.
- Every accepted non-code candidate improves or preserves the predeclared real-task gates with no new critical regression; a losing candidate is reverted from the candidate diff.
- Full Task/Skiller/Harness affected validation, provider parity, three-project boundary check and independent final review GREEN.
