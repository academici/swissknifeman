## What was presented to the owner before the choice

Verified repo facts (`findings/P5.2-task-description-gap-2026-08-06.md`):

1. The frozen D105 admission's `task` object carries **no natural-language task description** —
   twenty fields, none of them a statement of what to do.
2. The frozen P5.1 catalog **does** carry it (`scenario`, `kind`, `historical_patch_sha256`);
   `build-trial-admissions.py:158-181` silently drops all three when minting the admission.
3. `activity.BusinessProviderActivityPort._prompt` therefore cannot state the task — its only
   input is `task["files"]`.
4. At the frozen base `bde07fb0`, slice-01's two and slice-03's three declared files **do not
   exist**. The closing finding's claim that they were "already complete, terminal, tested bytes"
   described the current tree, not the disposable worktree the provider saw.
5. Adding a description field to the admission is a frozen-contract edit ("Unknown fields …
   refuse before intent") with a 14-admission re-mint cascade.
6. The activity port already resolves a frozen plan artifact by path at dispatch time (the output
   schema), and every admission already pins `task.catalog_hash` — so hash-verified catalog
   resolution needs no admission or contract change.

External corroboration (`RAG:✅ 2026-08-06`, two Perplexity Deep Research passes over
`claude-code-action`, OpenHands, SWE-agent, the SWE-bench harness, Crystal, Uzi, claude-squad,
vibe-kanban, container-use, FleetCode): **no surveyed system dispatches a coding task as a file
list without a natural-language description**, and the documented outcome of an under-specified
prompt is exactly ours — *"a no-op/empty diff that is still marked 'success'"*. Every component of
the Artifex construction is shipped somewhere in OSS; the assembly under one auditable authority
chain is what is rare.

Consequence presented: 0/3 is a **null measurement, not a negative one**, and P5.3/P5.4 consume the
same description-free carrier shape — so P5.4's economic verdict would measure our carrier gap and
spend five more one-shot D105 rows before the `2026-08-12` expiry.
