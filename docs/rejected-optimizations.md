Catalog of ideas that **look like an improvement**, have been considered consciously and **rejected**
(not «deferred until trigger» — there the idea will wait for its moment; here is the idea
tested and not suitable under current repo framework). Target: agent, having come across the same thought
again, finds here a ready-made verdict instead of reinventing and rediscovering the same
reasons for refusal.

> **Rule:** declined the offer (yours or someone else's) for a non-trivial reason that may
> resurface - **add the line here**: what they offered, why not, what we do instead.
> Difference from deferred ideas: there - «not now, criterion X will include»; here - «no, and here's why
> structurally, until the repo framework itself changes».

## How to use it

- Planning a feature/optimization that smells «why not just...» — check the table
  to RAG/design: if the line already exists, do not re-open the solution, just refer to it.
- The reason for refusal is tied to **current repo framework** (stdlib-only, subprocess without cross-imports,
  human-gate for promotions, and so on from `docs/philosophy.md`). If the frames themselves
  are changed consciously - the old line is not «hangs forever», it can be closed with a new entry with a link
  to a solution that changed the premise.
