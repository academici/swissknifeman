**Implementation Rules:** First publish `research/P3-owner-authority-threat-model.md` with assets, principals, entry points, STRIDE threats, provider support matrix and explicit security-boundary/workflow-guard classification. The same principal that is being constrained may not control attestation issuer keys/channel, nonce store or activation marker. Normalize attestations with exact required fields from P1 (at minimum issuer/provider, session/turn, command+arguments, cwd, plan/item/action digest, issued/expiry, nonce and signature/MAC where a real trust root exists); unknown/missing fields fail closed. **Code Guidance:** Keep provider-neutral policy separate from adapters. Prefer a small durable store/lock primitive; do not build a signing service unless an existing provider capability supplies a real out-of-principal trust channel. Test the representative forgery, replay, partial-activation and valid workflow paths. If no provider channel exists, ship the honest workflow-guard classification and fail-closed protected mode.
