## Reconciliation

P3.3 must author a two-provider crosswalk against the neutral target and prove that one-field
mutations cannot become zero, success or inferred equivalence. The contract alone provides the
normative vocabulary but not the executable mutation corpus. The case bank alone is synthetic
evidence and cannot define the contract. The validator is needed to expose how those mutations,
closed sets and provider evidence rows are mechanically adjudicated. The aggregate manifest and
validator prove identity/hash/dependency fan-in only, so they remain later integrity checks.

The finite read order is therefore: P3 design/remediation synthesis → defect/runtime findings →
P1.4 contract → P1.4 cases → P1.4 validator → the two provider implementations/tests → P0
provider evidence → aggregate P1 and provider-routing manifests. This lets the executor derive
provider rows from a frozen neutral target before seeing code-specific convenience shapes.
