## Решение

P5.4's forward path is decomposed into five items in execution order **P5.6 → P5.7 → P5.5 → P5.1 → P5.4** (`research/39-p5.4-forward-topology.md` §4), and the coupling surface is named up front. New item **P5.6** consolidates the generation-aware repair of the four frozen gates that are red on the current tree for one shared cause — `artifacts/P5/validate-shadow.py`, `artifacts/P3/validate-package-promotion.py`, `artifacts/P5/validate-repository-pilot.py` and the `124df816` P4 evidence anchor (F1/F2/F4/F5) — applying the pattern D121/D122 rule (6) already established for `validate-trial-admissions.py`. New item **P5.7** freezes `economy-evidence-v2` under D123 and re-cuts the aggregate contract manifest as an additive `contract-manifest-v2.json` enumerating all eight contracts, which also closes F3 (D108's own additive `business-dispatch-admission-v1.md` reads as directory drift to the frozen six-contract aggregate). **P5.5** reopens a third time as the sole producer of package bytes (D119) to export measured provider economy — the seven `economy-evidence-v2` metric fields as integers, with `inclusion_basis`/`zero_basis` where the rule applies — and to re-freeze both G2B manifests; its scope is inside D108's existing `exact scope` list (`providers/{codex,claude_code}.py`, `activity.py`, `cli.py` and their tests), so no new grant is created and, as D119 proved, the re-freeze runs with zero provider processes. **P5.1** reopens a third time as the sole carrier producer (D121) to mint the additive v3 set for D125's ten rows. Rejected: four separate reopens of P5.1/P3.8/P1.5/P4.7 for the gate repairs (reopening two closed phases to change plan-local validator scripts costs more bookkeeping than it buys, and P1.5's own reopen is required for D123 anyway, where it is scoped to the contract, not to the validators); and letting P5.4 repair its own gates or mint its own carriers (measurement self-certification, banned by D119/D121). **Coupling surface (files touched by ≥2 of these items):** `artifacts/P5/validate-repository-pilot.py` (P5.6 repairs it; P5.4 re-runs it as a gate), `artifacts/P1/validate-economy-evidence.py` and `contract-manifest-v2.json` (P5.7 authors, P5.4 consumes), `artifacts/P5/business-successor-{generation,assurance}-v1.json` (P5.5 re-freezes, P5.1 and P5.4 pin), `artifacts/P5/validate-trial-admissions.py` (P5.1 extends for v3, P5.4 runs), `phases/P5.md` (every item). **Consequences:** P5.5 and P5.1 lose their `🟠 Done with deviations` terminality for the third time and must re-earn it, phase P5 grows from five items to seven, and the plan's earliest possible `GO` moves behind a frozen-contract freeze — the cost of not having discovered F9/F10 at design time. Each blocker is fixed by the item that already owns the surface it lives on, so no item authors evidence about itself; consolidating the four same-cause gate repairs into one item avoids reopening two closed phases for mechanical work.
