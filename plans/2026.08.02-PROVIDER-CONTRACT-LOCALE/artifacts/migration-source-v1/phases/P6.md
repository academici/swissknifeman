# P6 — Configure Brain and fleet health/parity gates

## Phase Context

P6 turns the migrated provider state into an operational, evidence-backed health boundary.
It consumes terminal P0 recovery evidence, frozen P1/P3 status contracts, P4 acceptance and
P5 migrated/unresolved outcomes; none exists yet, so execution remains closed until their
committed identities are available. Brain is repaired as one protected, owner-approved rollback
domain rather than as a special-case bulk update. A versioned health contract then composes
package-owned Skiller, Harness, P3/P4 and mAInd evidence without duplicating their semantics.
The terminal fleet scan is exact-set and per-provider: missing, unsupported, unresolved or stale
rows remain non-green. A complete non-green report may finish P6 evidence work, but cannot open a
P7 release without a separate explicit exception. Items are sequential and do not declare a
workflow.

P6.2 is still one sequential item, but its root/core, Skiller, Harness and mAInd slices are
executed through D39 `roadmap.md` per-cwd sessions. The root coordinator alone owns the health
contract, integration manifest and close; package sessions emit scoped evidence and cannot
write another package or plan status.

**Scope Included:** exact owner-gated Brain repair and rollback; machine-readable skill-dependency
closure; provider-specific settings/trust and capability checks; versioned project/fleet health
composition; complete redacted two-run fleet evidence with bounded memory round-trip probes.

**Scope Excluded:** implicit credential rotation/revocation or full-access enablement; provider
semantics duplicated outside P1/P3; direct provider-file parsing as canonical intent; repair of
P5 unresolved rows; irreversible quarantine deletion; release/tag/push; a GREEN claim based on
package conformance, migration status, one provider or a subset of projects.

> **Contract of the phase (contract-first).** Consumes: terminal P0 recovery/security evidence, frozen P1/P3 provider contracts and status APIs, P4 acceptance bundle, P5 migrated/unresolved exact sets · Produces: owner-gated Brain repair result, frozen `provider-health-v1`, package-owned health surfaces and minimized exact-set fleet evidence · Boundaries: no implicit security authority, duplicated provider semantics, repair during measurement or release claim over required non-green rows.
