## 2. The measured-field gap decides the shape

`economy-evidence-v1`'s seven `metric_fields` × two envelopes (`worker`, `coordinator`) × three
lanes is the acceptance surface. What the three lanes can actually supply after the owner's Q1/Q2
selections:

| Envelope | candidate (Artifex + claude-code) | baseline (direct headless claude-code) |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `worker` | six measured integers from the provider result record (`usage.{input_tokens, cache_read_input_tokens, output_tokens}`, `total_cost_usd`, `duration_ms`, `num_turns`); `reasoning_tokens` definitionally inside `output_tokens` | identical surface, identical extraction code — that is the point of making the baseline a *headless* lane rather than a human one |
| `coordinator` | Artifex itself: a deterministic scheduler that makes **no model calls** (D3 — LLM calls are activities behind the side-effect boundary), so all seven are **structural zeros**, measured and provable, not imputed | the plan-local runner: likewise no model calls, all seven structural zeros |

Two consequences that shape every item below:

1. **The comparison is worker-vs-worker plus a provable coordinator-zero on both sides.** That is
   a *weaker* claim than "supervisor saves coordinator context" — and it is the honest one: this
   design cannot claim a coordinator saving it does not measure, and D7 already forbade calling
   context isolation a saving.
2. **A structural zero must be distinguishable from an unknown.** `economy-evidence-v2` has to
   carry that distinction explicitly (a declared `zero_basis` per envelope), otherwise the
   adjudicator cannot tell "measured 0 because no model call exists" from "we did not look" —
   which is exactly the failure D25 froze.

## 3. `economy-evidence-v2` — the minimum change, and why it is MAJOR

v1's own change policy classifies an added/changed **unknown rule** as MAJOR. The v2 delta is
exactly three rules; nothing else — no new field, no new formula, no new threshold, no new
verdict, no relaxed floor:

1. **Provider-structural inclusion.** A metric field the provider surface does not report
   *separately* because it is definitionally contained in another reported field is a MEASURED
   value of `0`, carrying a mandatory `inclusion_basis` entry naming the containing field and the
   evidence for the containment. Instantiated exactly once: claude-code `reasoning_tokens` ⊂
   `output_tokens` (RAG:✅ 2026-08-06, `findings/P5.4-metric-surface-gap-2026-08-06.md` F10). Any
   other absent field remains `null` + `unknown_reasons` under v1's untouched rule.
2. **Structural coordinator zero.** An envelope belonging to a component that makes no model call
   is a measured all-zero envelope carrying a mandatory `zero_basis` reference to the code path
   that proves it. Not a general licence: the basis must be a committed file reference, and a
   coordinator that *could* call a model but did not is still `unknown`.
3. **Single-provider economic scope.** Already legal in v1 (`provider_scope.mode =
   single-provider` «eligible only with a non-empty prior Owner D#»); v2 only pins that the
   reason-code set and the terminal verdict are unchanged by it, so a single-provider `GO` is
   never reported as parity.

Deliberately NOT in v2: cost imputation from a price sheet (D25, and P5.4's Scope Excluded both
forbid it) — which is why codex, whose CLI reports no cost at all, cannot be rescued by rule 1
and is instead removed from the economic lane by the owner's Q4 answer.

## 5. What P5.4 loses and what it keeps

**Loses:** provider parity as a terminal-success condition (P3.3 and P5.3 already carry provider
parity evidence on their own; P5.4 stops being its carrier), the codex lane, the `live-manual` /
`live-harness-run` / `live-workflow` baseline-lane vocabulary, and the D14-branch-2 (Workflow)
comparison, which the owner did not select and for which no coordinator-envelope carrier exists.

**Keeps, unchanged:** the D14 thresholds themselves (`<25%` intervention, `≥2×`, `N=5`), the
reliability/security floors evaluated before economics, the ban on post-hoc task/baseline/threshold
replacement, the ban on price lookup and imputation, the `NO-GO`-stays-open rule, and G3 pinning
the exact measured G2B digest.
