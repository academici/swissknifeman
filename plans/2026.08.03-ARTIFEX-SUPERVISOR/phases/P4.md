# P4 — Prove recovery, isolation, security, and observability

## Phase Context

P4 attacks the terminal P3 candidate at every durable, provider, Git, validation and reporting
boundary before any business pilot is allowed. It freezes an independent fault bank first, proves
no-rerun recovery, adds one owned linked-worktree write lane, tests authority/privacy boundaries,
and closes with a journal-derived owner/telemetry packet plus aggregate assurance validator.

**Scope Included:** plan-local fault injection and evidence, crash/reconcile fixes in the existing
candidate owners, controlled temporary-repository worktree execution/integration, shared candidate
security gate, owner attention/terminal reporting and independent P4 validators.

**Scope Excluded:** P0–P3 execution shortcuts; live business slices or rollout; changes under
`packages/**`, `core/**`, provider/fleet/Brain/generated/docs/release state; hook repair; real secret
capture; package/root CLI promotion; mAInd publication; economy GO/NO-GO.

> **Контракт фазы (contract-first).** Потребляет: terminal P3 adapters/manifest, terminal P2 candidate/manifest and all frozen P0/P1 threat/acceptance/contracts · Производит: exact fault bank, no-rerun recovery evidence, controlled worktree integration policy/evidence, security evidence, owner telemetry and aggregate assurance manifest · Границы: plan-local candidate and controlled fixtures/worktrees only; no business rollout, package/fleet mutation, hook repair or economic verdict.

Items execute strictly P4.1→P4.5. No workflow is declared: P4.2 consumes the P4.1 injector,
P4.3 consumes the proven reconcile semantics, P4.4 attacks the integrated candidate and P4.5 is
the aggregate gate. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `research/10-p4-assurance-design.md`).
