### P2.2 — Implement hash-chained run store, atomic snapshot, lock, and replay

**Intent:** Implement the strict single-writer `supervisor-run-v1` store whose append-only hash chain is the only recovery authority and whose snapshots/owner queue are disposable derived views. RAG:— (repo-grounded: D9–D11/D33 and `research/03-durable-state-and-recovery.md`).
**Why:** Tolerant JSONL reads, snapshot authority or unverified append/replace ordering can turn corruption or a crash into duplicate side effects or false acceptance. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `findings/06-p2-runtime-recon-2026-08-03.md`).
**Scope Included:** Immutable `run.json`; canonical record bytes; sequence/previous/record hashes; POSIX non-blocking one-writer lock with metadata; append flush/fsync/reopen/tail verification; strict streaming replay; idempotency/duplicate detection; temp+fsync+replace snapshot and derived owner queue; observed directory-fsync capability/limitation; permissions/containment; injected cut-point/write/lock/corruption tests.
**Scope Excluded:** Tolerant skip/repair, snapshot/session/process as SSOT, automatic truncated-tail deletion, provider/public side effect, plan journal rewrite, Windows portability claim, physical power-loss guarantee, new dependency or edits to generic `core.io` primitives.
