**Implementation Rules:** `plan_evidence` derives the graph from committed specs, bundles, decisions, producer-readiness and declared Files; there is no handwritten duplicate map. Spec inputs must precede product execution. A real read/write collision returns `EXECUTION_INPUTS_NOT_ANCESTOR` or `SELF_INVALIDATING_EVIDENCE_ANCHOR` before journal/product mutation and prescribes the minimum product/closure split; the compiler collapses anchors when no collision exists. Closure proves product paths byte-identical to product anchor and only verified terminal evidence can mark a Deliverable produced. Audit-owned journal/view/handoff/bundle paths classify as bookkeeping unless they also carry declared product semantics. `audit-finalization/v1` regenerates owned views once, rebuilds the candidate, consumes P7.1 lifecycle selection plus the P3.1 impact mode and records exact close admission before `audit-green`; unknown graph impact gathers evidence or owner review, never full.
