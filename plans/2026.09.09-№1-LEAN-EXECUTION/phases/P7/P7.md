# P7 — Whole-plan design completion circuit breaker

## Phase Context

P7 closes the policy seam exposed by this plan's own first durable whole-plan design audit. That
audit returned ATTENTION with four bounded findings; the findings were repaired, but the canonical
`plan-design finish` path still forced the changed carrier set back into another full design audit.
P7 extends the proportional evidence and impact rules produced by P3 to the design→execution
boundary: a bounded remediation with resolved findings may proceed directly to its routed execution
batch, while a genuinely new broad, frozen/public, security, safety or authority risk remains gated.
RAG:— (repo-grounded: `findings/12-design-audit-2026-09-09.md`,
`findings/13-design-audit-remediation.md`, `findings/14-repeat-design-audit-root-cause.md`, D13,
the canonical Task commands and task-development-philosophy).

**Scope Included:** whole-plan design-audit continuation policy; bounded-remediation closure;
owner-directed direct execution; exact new-material-risk trigger; handoff/delivery/lint integration;
provider projections, focused regressions and Task/Skiller/docs qualification.
**Scope Excluded:** deleting all audit capability; bypassing unresolved blocker/major findings or
frozen/public/security/safety/authority boundaries; weakening item Validation or phase closure;
changing historical audit events; consumer rollout, release, tag, push or deployment.
