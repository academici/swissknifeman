## `LoopBudget.from_committed` (review_policy.py) vs `authority.observed_rounds` (task_contract.build)

Two distinct, non-duplicate reconstructions from the same two committed carriers (`journal.jsonl` +
`## Audit Pn` blocks), at two different granularities:

- `task_contract._count_audit_rounds` — **phase-level** count of audit-round blocks, feeds
  `authority.observed_rounds` (compared against `audit_rounds_max`).
- `review_policy.LoopBudget.from_committed` — **per-finding** occurrence count (seam-locus text
  match across both carriers), feeds `same_finding_max` — the F5 fix: three independent
  `plan-audit` sessions must not each see `occurrences == 0` for the identical recurring finding.

## Anti-padding (Implementation Rules (2))

`build` raises `TaskContractBuildError(item_id, field, message)` — never a fabricated value — when
a schema-required field is empty (`"—"`) or a required split yields zero clauses. Proven by a
negative fixture: an item whose `Validation` is `"—"` fails `build` itself (no acceptance entries
to construct), and a schema-valid-but-thin contract (real item, unresolved `Validation` split)
fails `pre_final` with `ACCEPTANCE_NOT_PUBLIC` — the direct check Implementation Rules (2) demands.
