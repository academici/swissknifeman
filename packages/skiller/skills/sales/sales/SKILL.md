# Sales Super-Skill

The complete sales operating system — Perplexity Computer's 6 built-in sales skills
merged with Claude Code's PM/UX/brand-voice/content frameworks; full cycle from
prospecting to close. The full ~90KB playbook — templates, output formats, question
banks, scoring rubrics — lives in [references/full.md](references/full.md). This file
is a navigator only.

**Rule:** open `references/full.md` targeted — one section per concrete task.
Never load it whole: it will not fit a sane context budget.

## Section map (references/full.md)

- [How to Trigger This Skill](references/full.md#how-to-trigger-this-skill) — trigger phrases per workflow.
- [1. Gap Analysis Table](references/full.md#section-1-gap-analysis-table) — what Perplexity vs Claude Code contributes per capability.
- [2. Account Research & Prospect Intelligence](references/full.md#section-2-account-research--prospect-intelligence) — company/person research workflow + "Research:" output template (Quick Take, Profile, Key People, Qualification Signals, Recommended Approach).
- [3. Call Prep & Meeting Readiness](references/full.md#section-3-call-prep--meeting-readiness) — meeting-type-specific prep (discovery/demo/negotiation/QBR), discovery question banks, "Call Prep:" template with agenda and objections.
- [4. Competitive Intelligence & Positioning](references/full.md#section-4-competitive-intelligence--positioning) — battlecards, talk tracks, landmine questions, win/loss, brand-voice positioning.
- [5. Outreach & Content Creation](references/full.md#section-5-outreach--content-creation) — research-first AIDA outreach, "Outreach Draft:" template (email + LinkedIn backup, follow-up sequence, brand-voice validation).
- [6. Sales Assets & Deliverables](references/full.md#section-6-sales-assets--deliverables) — 7-phase asset creation (landing pages, decks, one-pagers), content repurposing matrix.
- [7. Deal Prioritization & Pipeline Management](references/full.md#section-7-deal-prioritization--pipeline-management) — RICE scoring adapted for deals, "Daily Briefing" template (#1 priority, numbers, meetings).
- [8. Unique Perplexity Computer Capabilities](references/full.md#section-8-unique-perplexity-computer-capabilities) — live-data/enrichment/CRM-dependent workflows.

Pick the section by task: research a company → 2 · prep a call → 3 · against a
competitor → 4 · write outreach → 5 · build an asset → 6 · rank deals / daily brief → 7.
