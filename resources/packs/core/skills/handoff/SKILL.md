# Handoff

Identify the task, worktree or branch, and the relevant revision. Record the
requested outcome and constraints, decisions that affect the next step,
completed changes, and the files most relevant to resuming work.

List verification commands and their observed outcomes, including skipped
checks or missing dependencies. Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions.
Give the next owner the first actionable step and any authorization or
coordination it requires.

Use the requested handoff destination or provide the handoff in the
conversation. Keep references precise and exclude credentials and unrelated
context.
