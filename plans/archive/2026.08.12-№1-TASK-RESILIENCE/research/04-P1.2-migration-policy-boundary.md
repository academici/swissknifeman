## Synthesis

The first Routing failure has a deterministic local repair: split a comma-separated legacy
`Items` cell into ordered rows only when every token is already a legal single item or ascending
same-phase range; preserve every other cell and never widen the closed runtime grammar. This fits
the applied owner action. RAG:— (repo-grounded: findings/05 and current parser/compiler).

That repair is not an executable specification for the fleet. The remaining classes require
policy, not syntax: whether and how to synthesize a missing journal, preserve an unknown legacy
result, project terminal fields, convert prose/default Routing, represent a phase without items,
repair read-budget and stale handoff carriers, and authorize an apply workflow after rehearsal.
Each choice affects resumability or external plan bytes. Silent defaults would violate the
lossless-migration and evidence-integrity contract. RAG:— (repo-grounded: findings/05, D7,
`packages/task/docs/plan-migration-runbook.md`).

## Alternatives

1. **Phase decomposition (recommended).** Keep a narrow compatibility item, add a policy/write
   item for the enumerated legacy classes and fleet application, then renumber the existing
   retirement item. This makes policy acceptance visible before external writes and keeps the
   removal gate dependent on an exact zero-live ledger.
2. **Single expanded P1.2.** Put normalization, legacy-state policy, controlled write workflow and
   all 15 migrations into one item. It saves one phase handoff but couples open design decisions,
   package code, external writes and fleet validation into one large attempt.
3. **Routing-only repair.** Green only target 1 and retain the current P1.2 deliverable. Rejected:
   it knowingly leaves 14 targets and the in-place boundary unresolved while claiming fleet DoR.

## Decision needed

Owner authority is required to choose phase decomposition or the single expanded item and to pin
the transformation/write contract. Until then P1.2 remains parked; its current
`implementation/high · manual · full · B-P1-CUTOVER` routing remains correct and is not the
cause of the block. RAG:— (repo-grounded: findings/05, `plan.md` §3 Routing).
