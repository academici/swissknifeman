# Legal Super-Skill (navigator)

Unified legal-operations reference for in-house legal teams. The full body
(~2100 lines) lives in **`references/full.md`** — open it **selectively, per
section anchor for the specific question at hand; never load it whole**.

> Operations-side skill. For design-time legal/compliance checklists during
> architecture work, see `architect/legal-compliance`.

## Core principle

Every legal output — redline, NDA classification, risk memo, compliance
response, meeting brief — must be specific, evidence-backed, actionable, and
verified (Section 8) before delivery.
