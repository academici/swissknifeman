## Run-report path evidence

`run-report.py` resolves `git rev-parse --show-toplevel` from observed `cwd` before writing a new
report. A nested Git-cwd fixture writes exactly one report at `<git-root>/.claude/runs/`, retains
the nested directory in the report's `cwd`, and verifies no nested `runs/` report is created.
Outside Git, the output remains `<cwd>/.claude/runs/`. SessionEnd remains best-effort and
nonblocking; historical reports are neither moved nor deleted.
