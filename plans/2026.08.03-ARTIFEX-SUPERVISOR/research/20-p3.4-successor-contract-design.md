## Forward-only topology

The selected path adds one contract and one provider-neutral implementation seam; it does not
rewrite the five frozen v1 contracts or reopen their historical item records. P1.6 freezes
`dispatch-admission-v1`, its independent cases/validator, and one content-addressed P3 smoke grant
bounded by owner decision D74. It also regenerates the P1 aggregate contract manifest from five to
six entries. P2.6 implements that contract as a pure manifest+grant+usage projection, updates the
authority loader to consume the admitted grant rather than the P0 unsigned example, and adds a
provider-free `dispatch` CLI path whose default activity remains a refusal. P3.4 then supplies the
bounded process activity and explicit provider selection. RAG:— (repo-grounded: sources above).

This supersedes D73's tentative wording about reopening P1.2/P1.3 and P2.4/P2.5. New items are the
safer carrier: completed item specs, commits, Completion Notes and frozen bytes stay historical;
all new semantics have one producer and one implementation owner. The affected P1/P2 phases still
reopen because their output sets grow, and their old Phase Handoffs become stale until P1.6/P2.6
close. RAG:— (repo-grounded: plan-protocol phase-reopen canon and current phase files).

## `dispatch-admission-v1` boundary

The contract owns exactly the composition missing from the current graph:

1. Pin the complete `slice-manifest-v1`, `authority-policy-v1`, `worker-envelope-v1`, provider
   parity and run-state hashes; copied values never replace those owners.
2. Admit one plan/item/run/slice/attempt/idempotency identity and one provider choice against an
   immutable grant. The grant pins `owner_decision=D74`, the plan hash, provider allowlist, route,
   files/tools/validations, numeric budget maxima and `max_slices=1`; it is limited to P3 smoke and
   grants no P5 business run.
3. Define a total projection into the existing scheduler slice and worker request: dependencies,
   source pins, route/capabilities/permissions, complete `attempts/tokens/cost/duration/turns`
   limits, durable usage counters, and authority predicates. Usage comes from replay, never from a
   caller-provided reset.
4. Refuse missing/unsigned/wrong-plan/wrong-item/stale grants, provider/route/scope/validation
   widening, unknown hard budgets, source or parity drift, replay/usage mismatch, more than one
   slice, and any contract/hash contradiction before intent or process launch.
5. Preserve the sequence `verify admission → authority allow → durable intent → one injected
   activity → normalized observation → deterministic validation`; the activity never accepts,
   retries or mutates authority.

The P0 `owner-mandate-input-v1.json` remains compatibility/test input and is never a live provider
grant. A nullable token/cost field remains unknown, not unlimited; the P3 smoke grant supplies exact
finite numeric caps, while a future business run needs its own owner-approved grant. RAG:—
(repo-grounded: frozen contract semantics and the reproduced conflict).

## CLI and ownership

P2.6 adds a provider-neutral `dispatch --run-id ... --admission ...` surface. It reopens the run,
re-verifies the content-addressed admission and current source pins, derives usage from the durable
journal, and invokes the scheduler. With no P3 activity injected it deterministically returns
`provider_unavailable`; it imports no provider module and starts no subprocess. Existing `start`
continues to compile/freeze only, and `resume` continues to inspect/reconcile only.

P3.4 extends only this dispatch seam with `--provider codex|claude-code --max-slices 1`, selects an
already conformed adapter, and runs one bounded fresh process. Omission preserves P2's refusal.
Neither P3.4 nor the adapter may construct authority input, budget maxima or grant fields locally.
RAG:— (repo-grounded: terminal P2/P3 module boundaries and D74).

## Rejected alternatives

- Editing the frozen v1 contracts in place: destroys the meaning of their terminal commits and
  aggregate evidence.
- Reopening four historical items and overwriting Completion Notes: creates two competing accounts
  of what those item IDs delivered.
- Building the projection inside P3.4: lets provider code invent scheduler authority/budgets.
- Teaching `start` or `resume` to dispatch implicitly: conflates freeze/recovery with a side-effect
  boundary and makes replay ambiguous.
- Treating the P0 unsigned example or a CLI JSON field as owner authority: spoofable and wider than
  the recorded decision.

## Execution order and stop conditions

`P1.6 → close P1 → P2.6 → close P2 → P3.4`. P1.6 stops if the new carrier cannot remain additive
to all five frozen contracts. P2.6 stops if any projected field lacks one frozen producer or if
provider-free dispatch can reach a real process. P3.4 stops if the admitted grant, provider parity,
source pins or numeric caps do not verify exactly. No later step may bypass a nonterminal producer.
