# P3 — Proportional phase assurance and evidence reuse

## Phase Context

P3 makes phase audit and close proportional to declared impact and current material evidence. It
first establishes one assurance/impact policy, then repairs Routing-table consumption, makes item
validation receipts reusable, and closes the spec→product→closure evidence graph. It consumes D2,
D6–D8 and the P1/P2 admission, continuity and repair contracts so bookkeeping noise remains local
while public/security/live/authority risk stays fail-closed. Completion means every candidate diff
has one typed continuation, unchanged deterministic proof is reusable, live gates remain live, and
an audit cannot invalidate itself or announce GREEN before exact close admission.

**Scope Included:** phase assurance, audit subject/candidate identity, impact modes, Routing parser boundary, validation receipt reuse and budget, evidence anchors/dependency closure, audit finalization.
**Scope Excluded:** weakening item Validation or live/security gates; consumer-plan mutation; provider final transport (P4); arbitrary neighbouring cleanup; automatic full audit for unknown impact.

> **Phase Assurance | v1.**
> Impact: internal-integration
> Integration Seams: `phase-audit-policy` · `terminal-evidence-reuse` · `closure-anchor`
> Live or Irreversible: no
> Audit Intent: required
> Rationale: shared Task close policy, terminal evidence and provider projections change together and require one independent cross-item audit.

> **Контракт фазы (contract-first).** Потребляет: P1 batch/continuity + P2 repair/readiness · Производит: typed phase assurance + reusable evidence/anchor lifecycle · Границы: live/public/security/authority checks remain fail-closed and consumer plans are fixtures only.
