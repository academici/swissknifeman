## Решение

P0.8 repairs the three validation boundaries forward: Layout v2 removes the inline Update Log
from `plan.md` in favor of journal-backed `status.md`; stale bundles are regenerated only through
`plan-views.py` after the final correction event; and P0.6 replaces the full 11.7 MB classification
corpus in its model-facing declaration with the digest-qualified consumer projection in
`research/12-p0.8-validation-recovery-design.md` §3. The full corpus remains unchanged and is
still passed to the existing checker as a machine-only validation argument. One P0.6 correction
event records the post-close declaration repair. RAG:— (repo-grounded:
`findings/P0.8-validation-blocker-2026-08-10.md`,
`research/12-p0.8-validation-recovery-design.md`)

## Почему

An exception or threshold change would hide the invalid read graph. The compact projection is
the smallest truthful carrier for P0.6's observed consumer read-set, preserves the source digest
and classification policy, and leaves row-level semantic validation with P0.2.

## Consequences

P0.6's current spec is newer than its original terminal commit. Git, the P0.8 provenance ledger
and the correction event must therefore remain sufficient to reconstruct both declarations.
Any future P0.6 consumer that needs per-path rows invalidates this projection and requires a new
decision/read-graph design; the full corpus may not be silently reintroduced as model context.
