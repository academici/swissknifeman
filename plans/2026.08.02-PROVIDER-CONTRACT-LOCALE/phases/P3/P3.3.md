**Intent:** Implement the P1-defined adapter-scoped status store and deterministic diagnostics that turn P3.2 observations/readbacks into redacted ownership, drift, migration-readiness and aggregate provider state for P5–P7. RAG:— (repo-grounded: plan D4–D5/D8–D10, `core/provider/lock.py`, `findings/07-p3-control-plane-recon-2026-08-02.md`).
