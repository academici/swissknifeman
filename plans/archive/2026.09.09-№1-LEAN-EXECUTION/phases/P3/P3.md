# P3 — Proportional phase assurance and evidence reuse

## Phase Context

P3 makes phase audit and close proportional to declared impact and current material evidence. It
first establishes one assurance/impact policy, then repairs Routing-table consumption and closes
the spec→product→closure evidence graph. It consumes D2, D6, D14 and the completed P7 lean
lifecycle so validation selection is not reimplemented as the retired P3.3 receipt programme.
Bookkeeping noise remains local while public/security/live/authority risk stays fail-closed.
Completion means every candidate diff has one typed continuation and an audit cannot invalidate
itself or announce GREEN before exact close admission.

**Scope Included:** phase assurance, audit subject/candidate identity, impact modes, Routing parser boundary, evidence anchors/dependency closure, audit finalization over P7 lifecycle results.
**Scope Excluded:** weakening item Validation or live/security gates; consumer-plan mutation; provider final transport (P4); arbitrary neighbouring cleanup; automatic full audit for unknown impact.
