### P3.3 — Proportional validation evidence reuse

**Intent:** Replace prose-only terminal Validation evidence with digest-bound reusable receipts and a bounded full/delta/gate-rebind audit budget so unchanged deterministic proof is reused while live checks remain fresh. RAG:— (repo-grounded: artifacts/source-roadmap/2026.08.14-repeat-audit-evidence-reuse-budget.md; decisions/D2-proportional-validation.md; decisions/D8-one-assurance-evidence-lineage.md).
**Why:** `terminal-evidence/v1` records only spec SHA plus carrier/status/evidence IDs and cannot prove command, input, product, tool, route, projection or lifetime identity; current audit policy therefore re-executes unchanged commands and historical deviations can trigger repeated full audits. RAG:— (repo-grounded: findings/04-p3-design-baseline.md; packages/task/lib/task/terminal_policy.py:39-129; artifacts/source-roadmap/2026.08.14-repeat-audit-evidence-reuse-budget.md:48-101).
**Scope Included:** `validation-receipt/v2` closed schema and canonical command ID/argv; spec/product/input/tool/runtime/route/projection digests; exit/result/evidence/negative-fixture IDs; deterministic/live/security-live/external-live risk classes; timing and reuse lifetime; terminal-policy/journal/state binding; exact reuse predicate; full/delta/gate-rebind execution plan and report; second-full justification and third-full owner gate; executed/reused counts/wall time/token estimate; legacy one-baseline fallback; command/lint/delivery/returns/provider integration.
**Scope Excluded:** Caching live evidence across its boundary; reusing receipt after any bound identity changes; weakening item Validation; automatic threshold drift after telemetry; inventing dependency edges (P3.4); external telemetry service or rollout.

**Implementation Rules:** `plan_validation` is the only owner of receipt validation and reuse. Reuse requires exact spec/product/input/tool/runtime/route/projection identity and a green deterministic result; any mismatch yields an invalid reason and execution, not guessed reuse. Live/security-live/external-live receipts use `reusable_until=never` and rerun once immediately before the declared verdict/close boundary without invalidating deterministic coverage. One material lineage gets one normal full baseline; a second requires `majority-material-change|central-contract-change|evidence-graph-untrusted|owner-requested` evidence from P3.1/P3.4, and a third returns `AUDIT_FULL_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` without owner D#. Writer enforcement is forward-only and existing `terminal-evidence/v1` stays readable via one conservative baseline. RAG:— (repo-grounded: decisions/D8-one-assurance-evidence-lineage.md; artifacts/source-roadmap/2026.08.14-repeat-audit-evidence-reuse-budget.md:85-163).
**Code Guidance:** Build the pure receipt validator/reuse predicate first, then wire terminal and audit policy. Do not cache prose `green`, partial rows, failed commands or live checks. Canonicalize argv without secrets and bind bounded stdout through evidence IDs, not raw unbounded logs. Use table-driven one-field mutation tests over every digest/risk/lifetime field and assert executed/reused accounting exactly. No special P0 logic: the fixture must pass through the generic lineage reducer. Keep the 50% threshold as one conservative signal, not the sole material-risk definition; thresholds never self-tune from the first-ten telemetry.
