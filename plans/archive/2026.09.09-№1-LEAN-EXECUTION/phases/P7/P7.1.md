### P7.1 — Lean Task control loop consolidation

**Intent:** Replace the layered normal Task path with one runtime-owned lifecycle that captures admission once, runs changed-scope validation, updates only changed terminal carriers, persists one semantic Next and uses one scoped commit by default. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `findings/15-lean-lifecycle-overhead-2026-09-10.md`; `research/08-p7-lean-control-loop.md`; D14).
**Why:** The P2 audit repeated focused, full-package, provider, Skiller, docs and cross-plan checks, rebound terminal evidence and regenerated broad plan state after product completion; meanwhile the five canonical plan commands plus plan-protocol total 176,325 bytes with four copies of core algorithms. The overhead is owned by duplicated policy and unconditional gates, not one missing fast flag. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `findings/15-lean-lifecycle-overhead-2026-09-10.md`; `findings/16-p7-design-preflight-2026-09-10.md`).
**Scope Included:** One transient lifecycle-selection API/CLI composed from existing Task owners; route/context capture once per unchanged run; low-risk, bounded-integration, hard-risk and release/authority change classes; focused/affected/integration/fleet validation selection; unrelated-baseline delta; read-only audit with at most one deterministic bookkeeping repair and one exact owning continuation for product findings; selective one-pass state/status/decision/bundle/closure projection; one-commit default and two-commit boundary predicate; one provider-neutral semantic Next rendered natively by delivery; removal of unconditional design/audit fresh-root selection; at least 40% canonical command/canon byte reduction; generated provider projections, docs and changelog qualification.
**Scope Excluded:** A persisted lifecycle receipt, policy schema or alternate plan layout; new status/review/continuation kind; `lean=true`; a second parser/reducer for existing typed evidence; keyword-only risk detection; reusing live/security evidence past its boundary; audit product edits; silently hiding foreign-plan failures; weakening high-risk or release authority; hand-editing generated adapters/projections; changing historical plan events; external rollout/release/tag/push/deploy.

**Implementation Rules:** Existing modules remain the single owners of their typed contracts. If a thin `plan_lifecycle` composer is added, it may return only transient selection data and must delegate route, repair/block, impact/review, projection, continuity and delivery semantics to those owners; it may not persist a schema/carrier or duplicate their enums. The low-risk default is exact focused validation, no independent audit/fleet replay, changed projections once and one scoped commit. Widen deterministically for security/authentication, destructive migration, irreversible external action, payment/accounting, concurrency/shared state, public API/schema, low-confidence impact or explicit release/owner boundary. A foreign baseline is visible but non-gating unless its owner/dependency closure intersects the change. Audit performs zero product writes and zero second audits; a product finding returns one exact owning continuation, while at most one deterministic audit-owned bookkeeping repair may occur. Route/session evidence is reused until `route_change_boundary()` proves change. Design/audit command names alone do not select a fresh root. Canonical five-command plus plan-protocol bytes must be `<=105795`; provider adapters and bootstrap projections are regenerated, never edited. RAG:— (repo-grounded: D14; Task Development Philosophy; findings 15–16).
**Code Guidance:** Start with black-box fixtures for the complete low-risk path and every widening boundary, then delete duplicated command/canon algorithms and make commands reference the runtime owner calls. No special case for this plan, P2, ATTENTION or a filename. Do not turn prose words such as `security` into the sole classifier; use declared scope/Files/risk plus existing typed evidence, and fail closed only when impact stays low-confidence. Assert exact selected commands, projection count, audit/product-write count, commit count, context decision and semantic Next. Reuse the evidence-first pattern: observe → minimal reproduce → classify → act → verify; unrelated/pre-existing is a baseline delta, acceptance stays owning work, and safety/authority widens only after concrete evidence. Do not weaken tests to meet the byte budget; remove copied explanations and dead policy instead.
