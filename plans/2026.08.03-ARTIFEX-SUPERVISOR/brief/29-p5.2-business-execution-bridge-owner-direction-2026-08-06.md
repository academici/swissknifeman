# Owner direction — P5.2 business execution bridge — 2026-08-06

Repository owner direction in this session is to complete P5 through the
workflow. The owner therefore authorizes the smallest forward repair needed to
execute the three already frozen P5.2 admissions: a package-owned business
execution bridge that consumes only an already validated D105 admission and
the terminal G2B manifest.

The bridge may construct only the existing Codex/Claude business argv,
execute it once under the admission's fixed root/caps, retain minimized
normalized observations, and return a closed result to the P5.2 harness. It
must not mint/reset/rewrite an admission, run P5.3+, change G2/P4/P5.1/P5.5,
retry a provider, broaden provider/version/capability/permission scope, or
persist raw provider content. A failed bridge/provider invocation remains a
P5.2 blocking observation rather than a reason to fabricate evidence.
