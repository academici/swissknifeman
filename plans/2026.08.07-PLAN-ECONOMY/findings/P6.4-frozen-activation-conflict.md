# P6.4 — frozen activation conflict

Date: 2026-08-09
Actor: gpt-5.6-sol/high (`task:plan-run`)
Trigger: конфликт правил

## Reproduction

The required activation is declared as forward-only by an item's `Updated` date.
The dormant downstream check lives in `packages/task/lib/task/plan_downstream.py`,
outside P6.4's allowed implementation file `plan_gates.py`. A local, uncommitted
activation trial with disposition `activated_from: 2026-08-09` produced 311 new
`downstream-gate-v1` errors on the active plan.

## Cause

`packages/task/lib/task/plan_status.py:140–146` assigns the one global latest journal
date to every item row in `status.md`. Consequently every historic terminal producer
appears updated on 2026-08-09 once P6.4 starts. The specified predicate "skip items/
phases whose Updated is earlier than activated_from" cannot distinguish historic items
from newly closed items with the existing carriers.

## Required decision

> 🔴 ESCALATION P6.4: specify and freeze the authoritative per-item/phase forward-only
> timestamp carrier, then add its producer/consumer implementation scope before gate
> activation | trigger: конфликт правил | нужен: plan-design

Do not weaken the four gates, introduce a warning/opt-out flag, or use a global plan
timestamp as a substitute.
