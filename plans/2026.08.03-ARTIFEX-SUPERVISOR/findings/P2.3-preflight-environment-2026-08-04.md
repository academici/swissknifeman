- `packages/harness/bin/harness hook-check` returned `Неизвестная команда: hook-check`.
- `packages/maind/bin/maind health --cwd .` returned `ERROR: лишние аргументы: .`.

The supported equivalents passed on the same tree:

- `packages/harness/bin/harness doctor --target .` reported `harness: OK`, global hooks installed,
  and the route-evidence limitation that effort remains self-attested.
- `packages/maind/bin/maind health --cwd --json` reported `status: connected`, a successful memory
  round-trip, and green Codex/Claude provider trust.

This is a preflight-command projection defect, not a P2.3 runtime defect. P2.3 does not change
harness, maind, hooks, or their generated projections; the observation is retained for the owning
environment-canon maintenance path.
