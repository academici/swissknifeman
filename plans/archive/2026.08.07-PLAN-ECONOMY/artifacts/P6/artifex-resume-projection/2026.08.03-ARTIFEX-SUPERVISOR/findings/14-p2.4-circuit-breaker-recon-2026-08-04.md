## Reconciliation verdict

The missing literal is not a missing transition. Repeated-finding exhaustion is a retry-budget
breaker owned by `authority-policy-v1`; P2.4 must project its closed decision to the existing
`budget_exhausted` run event. The journal remains the authority: the event carries the triggering
slice/attempt identity and the exact closed P2.3 authority decision as minimized evidence. No
`append_intent` or activity call occurs on this path.

`BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` is run control state, so it is the global breaker: it blocks admission of every
slice, including otherwise independent work, until an explicit owner decision. “Global” therefore
does not require a second event or state. Per-slice identity remains in the triggering journal row
and its referenced decision evidence.

For a cap learned only after a boundary, the scheduler first records the contracted observation
and non-acceptance/rollback path; after replay returns to `READY`, durable counters cause the same
`budget_exhausted` transition. Emitting an undeclared breaker event from `BOUNDARY_ACTIVE` or
`OBSERVED` remains forbidden.
