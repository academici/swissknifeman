# ADR 0061: Run-policy packs, engine choice in `[orch]` and `orch.run.*` events

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Scenarios were files outside a module, the engine was fixed in code, and nothing told other modules that a run began or
ended.

## Decision

- **Packs.** A run-policy ships as a pack, `configs/run-policies/<id>/policy.json` with a `version` and `cases/*.json`
  (expected stages, refusals and decision for a fixture input). The shipped packs are `solo`, `scout-solo`,
  `frontend-bounded`, `review-only` and `full`. More folders come from `orch.profiles.dirs` (machine layer only).
  `orch.py policy list|show|test` reads and tests them.
- **Configuration.** `[orch]` (`urn:swissknifeman:orch:config:v1`, `configs/config.schema.json`) has `engine`
  (`native`, `cao`, `claude-only`), `engine_by_role`, `default_profile`, `work_source`, `capabilities.stale`
  (`warn` or `refuse`), `profiles.dirs` and `events.enabled`. `config.example.toml` is generated by `aicore`.
- **Choice.** `prepare` reads the configuration once and writes its digest and the values that affected the result into
  the launch plan. An engine without a capability a stage requires is refused with `CAPABILITY_UNSUPPORTED` and the
  chain of reasons; the choice is never silently degraded. `orch.py explain` reads the decision back.
- **Events.** `orch.run.started`, `orch.run.stage.finished`, `orch.run.finished` and `orch.run.refused`, declared in
  `provides.events` of `unit.json`, with closed `event-data/v1` schemas. Emission is best-effort, after the run journal
  is written; a failure prints `ORCH_EVENT_NOT_EMITTED` and changes neither output nor exit code.

## Alternatives considered

- Policies as free files: rejected, a policy without cases cannot be tested.
- A hard engine failure on emission errors: rejected, observation must not stop a run.

## Consequences

- Analyst and brain subscribe to the events without importing `orch`.
- A new pack is data plus cases, not code.

## Supersedes

None.
