2. Fixture expected values are literals from the frozen plan/route contract. They are not copied

   and present exactly once in `excluded_terminal` with `ITEM-TERMINAL`. It does not require any
   later item to remain non-terminal.
4. Production `observe_sources()` and `compile_slice()` remain unchanged unless the executor finds
   a test-only seam strictly necessary. Any proposal to admit terminal rows to compilation,
   synthesize route from `excluded_terminal`, or change Harness is a scope conflict and must stop.
