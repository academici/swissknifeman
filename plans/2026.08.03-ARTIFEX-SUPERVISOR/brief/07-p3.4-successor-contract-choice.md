# P3.4 owner choice — successor-contract — 2026-08-04

After the P3.4 bridge feasibility gate was explained, the owner explicitly accepted the
recommended `successor-contract` path. The live-provider objective remains in scope; the alternative
that would remove live dispatch from P3.4 and downstream P4/P5 claims is rejected.

This approval authorizes plan design for the smallest forward-only contract and implementation
chain needed to connect the frozen slice, authority, scheduler and worker boundaries. It does not
authorize implementation in this design session, implicit authority or budget defaults, rewriting
the historical contents or closure evidence of completed items, package/root/fleet rollout, or a
provider process before the successor chain is terminal.
