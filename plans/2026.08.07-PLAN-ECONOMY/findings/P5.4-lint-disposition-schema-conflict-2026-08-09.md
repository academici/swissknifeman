# P5.4 — conflict: lint-disposition registry shape

## Observation

`P5.4` requires its Validation to enumerate `d["checks"]` as rows with `id` and
`default_enabled`.  The committed P3.6 registry
`packages/task/configs/lint-check-dispositions.json` instead declares `checks` as a
mapping from fully qualified lint function name to a `{ "disposition": ... }`
object.  The required Validation expression therefore cannot execute against the
current registry.

## Why execution stops

Changing the registry to the list-shaped form required by the item would change the
live P3.6 lint-disposition contract, while retaining the mapping would require
changing P5.4's explicit acceptance command.  Neither choice is authorised by this
item's frozen Scope Included.

## Evidence

- Actual registry: `packages/task/configs/lint-check-dispositions.json`.
- Required validation: `phases/P5/P5.4.md` `Validation` field.
- Task Contract before mutation: `pre_mutation: allow` under
  `implementation/high` (`gpt-5.6-terra`).

## Required repair

`task:plan-design 2026.08.07-PLAN-ECONOMY P5.4` must choose and record the
compatible registry representation and then align the item Validation and Scope with
that decision.  The repair must preserve P3.6 consumers or deliberately migrate
them with explicit validation.
