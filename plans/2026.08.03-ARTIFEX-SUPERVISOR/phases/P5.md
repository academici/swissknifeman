# P5 — Run measured pilots and decide economics

## Phase Context

P5 turns the terminal P4 candidate into measured evidence without letting it choose tasks,
baselines or verdict rules after seeing results. It freezes archive mappings and all live trials,
obtains an exact owner grant over the hashed business request, materializes every per-trial
manifest/replay/admission before provider dispatch, then runs a reversible three-slice repository
pilot, a controlled recovery/provider-failure pilot and exactly five paired battle waves.

**Scope Included:** candidate-independent trial catalog and shadow replay; controlled disposable
repository pilots; exact owner-approved business grants and per-trial admissions; real fresh provider workers under frozen authority; crash/resume/duplicate/failure
evidence; matched manual/Workflow/`harness run` baselines; normalized economy/intervention metrics;
independent GO/NO-GO adjudication.

**Scope Excluded:** P0–P4 execution shortcuts; owner dirty-tree/business/fleet/Brain/package/root-CLI/
generated/docs/release mutation; archive edits or imports; raw prompts/transcripts/thinking/tool payloads/
environment values; credential/quota manipulation; post-hoc task/baseline/threshold replacement;
productization or release.

> **Контракт фазы (contract-first).** Потребляет: terminal exact-green admission-aware P4 assurance/candidate, frozen P0 baseline/rubric, terminal P1 six-contract set and P3 smoke carriers only as negative lineage · Производит: frozen trial/shadow evidence, owner-hash-bound business grants, deterministic per-trial manifest/replay/admission index, reversible three-slice/recovery pilots, five paired battle waves and independent GO/NO-GO · Границы: controlled disposable repositories and minimized evidence only; P3 smoke authority is never business authority; no archive/product/package/fleet/Brain/release mutation.

Items execute strictly P5.1→P5.4. No workflow is declared: every item consumes the previous
artifact, while the autonomous chain inside the pilot is the candidate under measurement, not a
second plan scheduler. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `research/11-p5-pilot-economics-design.md`).
