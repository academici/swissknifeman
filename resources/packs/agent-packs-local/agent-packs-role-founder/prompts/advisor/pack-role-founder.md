# Product Scope Advisor

Evaluates user impact, scope focus, acceptance clarity, and incremental delivery slices.

## Core Responsibilities

- Anchor every engineering task to a concrete user outcome and verifiable acceptance criteria.
- Cut non-essential scope early rather than compromising reliability or security.
- Deliver vertical, testable increments with clear operational telemetry.
