### P3.1 — Phase assurance and impact broker

**Intent:** Introduce one structured phase-assurance declaration and pure impact broker so first-audit admission and every post-GREEN candidate change select the smallest justified close, gate-rebind, delta, full, evidence-gathering or owner-review continuation. RAG:— (repo-grounded: artifacts/source-roadmap/2026.08.14-phase-assurance-contract.md; artifacts/source-roadmap/2026.08.14-phase-close-impact-broker.md; decisions/D8-one-assurance-evidence-lineage.md).
**Why:** Current policy treats `Routing.Review`, item count and any aggregate fingerprint mismatch as audit triggers, while bare `PHASE_AUDIT_STALE` cannot identify affected items or distinguish bookkeeping from product change; the same GREEN candidate can therefore be rejected or fully re-audited without a new material hypothesis. RAG:— (repo-grounded: findings/04-p3-design-baseline.md; packages/task/lib/task/plan_gates.py:342-398,486-542).
**Scope Included:** `Phase Assurance | v1` parser/schema and diagnostics; impact/audit-intent enums; canonical `phase-audit-subject/v1`; runtime-built candidate snapshot; `audit-impact/v1` field-level classifier; closed first-audit and post-GREEN action reducer; direct-close safety matrix; exact affected items/edges/reasons/continuation; journal, close/delivery/lint/returns and command/provider integration; forward legacy baseline; P1/P10/P11 fixtures.
**Scope Excluded:** Reusing unqualified terminal Validation; implementing spec/product/closure anchors (P3.4); automatic full on unknown impact; treating item count or historical status as material impact; weakening public/security/live/irreversible gates; changing consumer-plan history.

**Implementation Rules:** `plan_assurance.evaluate()` is the only owner of phase-assurance plus impact-to-action policy; callers build the candidate from `<plan-dir>, <phase-id>` and cannot pass arbitrary trusted scope. Assurance declaration cannot override non-green receipt, material risk or mandatory live/public/security/irreversible gates. `Review` remains item-review depth and item count is not an independent phase risk. Matching GREEN subject variants normalize to `Pn`. Unknown/unclassifiable impact returns evidence gathering or owner review, never implicit `full`. Post-GREEN modes are exactly `gate-rebind|delta|full`; full requires no prior baseline or a later closed justification from P3.3. Legacy plans without assurance receive a named conservative baseline continuation, not a permanent failure.
