## Решение

P7.1 uses one frozen plan-local `p7.1-skill-replay-admission/v1` carrier as the exact live replay
denominator. Its capability IDs are exactly the three applied P4.3 `semantic.skill` row IDs; each
is bound to canonical and provider-projection digests, minimum Skill Core, positive/negative/
boundary workflows, structured grader assertions and a finite non-core ablation inventory.
Frozen carrier SHA-256: `08f97239b4a79b7542f6a256a1017a57bec5e6db30f77b1d657288c368612062`.

The base matrix is exactly 54 rows: three capabilities × three workflows × two providers × three
variants (`no-skill`, `minimum-core`, `provider-tuned`). Provider-native discovery/invocation
remains adapter-owned; `provider-tuned` consumes the provider's generated bootstrap projection and
the same canonical body, never a provider fork. Missing/extra/duplicate/skipped rows, negative
false activation, worse-than-baseline results, no strict positive/boundary improvement, unproven
retained non-core prose, digest drift or provider masking remain release blockers.

RAG:— (repo-grounded: `findings/P7-provider-acceptance.md`,
`research/19-p7.1-replay-admission-design.md`, `configs/locale-corpus-v1.json`, accepted P4.3
manifest, D57/D58 and frozen skill evidence/adapter contracts).

## Почему

The P2 fixture is intentionally `structure-only` and cannot choose or prove live efficacy. A
frozen P7.1-local mapping closes the ambiguity without reopening terminal producers or letting
executor judgment/observed output define the acceptance set.

## Consequences

P7.1 must verify the admission carrier and referenced source/projection digests before the first
provider call, execute the exact matrix and ablations read-only, and remain non-green on missing
live evidence. P2/P4/P5/P6 semantics, the approved empty P5 WAL, provider/fleet state and release
authority remain unchanged.
