**Intent:** Produce a minimized, deterministic acceptance bundle that proves shared provider semantics by semantic capability ID and proves each provider-native strength against its own frozen support/limitation contract, without substituting byte equality or one-provider success. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `brief/00-brief.md`, `research/06-p6-health-parity-design.md`, `findings/11-p7-release-acceptance-recon-2026-08-02.md`)
